EN 

1 country out of 22.

Universal Music Africa covers 22 countries, from Senegal to Madagascar. Across that footprint, YouTube currently monetises music in only one: Senegal. Its MD, Franck-Alcide Kacou, said so in an interview with Stratégies.

In the other 21, a video can rack up millions of views without generating music ad revenue on YouTube.

The good news: progress is under way with Meta in Côte d'Ivoire, after lobbying by Universal and the Ministry of Culture. Monetisation is won country by country.
