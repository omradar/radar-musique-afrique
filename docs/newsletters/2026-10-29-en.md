# African Music Industry Weekly: Mechanical Royalties, Chart Dynamics, and Tour Adjustments

## Editorial

Rights administration and live infrastructure take center stage this week. From mechanical royalty collection mechanisms in South Africa to structural distribution questions in Liberia, publishing registration remains an urgent operational priority. Meanwhile, platform distribution partnerships continue to consolidate presence on digital charts, while live tour schedules across the region face unexpected calendar adjustments.

## Top stories

**South Africa's CAPASSO reports R269 million collected in a year**  
Mechanical rights hub CAPASSO processes streaming royalties through a 12.5% fee structure and quarterly payouts. Streams for unregistered works move into a three-year suspense account, after which unallocated funds are distributed to other registered writers. Rights holders must register works prior to the December distribution cycle to secure their payouts. Source: [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/capasso-south-africa-mechanical-royalties-streaming-three-year-suspense/).

**Liberian copyright levy administration under review**  
Liberia's 2016 IP Act mandates a levy on copying devices and grants performers half of airplay royalties. However, collective management organization COSOL has distributed these funds as welfare payments rather than per-song royalties. Performers and labels operate under distinct framework rules under the 70/20/10 split model. Source: [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/liberia-cosol-copyright-levy-welfare-performers-airplay-royalties/).

**EMPIRE secures heavy presence on Nigerian charts**  
Artists FOLA and Seyi Vibez, both backed by distributor EMPIRE, appear on eight of the top 15 tracks on Spotify Nigeria and seven entries in the TurnTable top 10. Industry metrics show varying trends when comparing pure digital stream volume against traditional broadcast radio reach for these titles. Source: [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/fola-seyi-vibez-nigeria-charts-streams-vs-radio/).

## Key takeaways

* Songwriters can avoid losing mechanical streaming income by ensuring catalog metadata is registered before CMO deadlines, such as CAPASSO's December cutoff.
* Rightsholders operating across West Africa may need to verify whether local collection societies disburse blank media levies as direct per-song royalties or general welfare funds.
* Tour management teams can protect live operations by defining explicit rescheduling and refund terms ahead of time to handle health-related delays.
