# The Payout Gap in Benin and the Rise of Francophone Live Power

## Editorial
This week highlights the persistent gap between legislation and revenue distribution, notably with Benin's decade-old private copy levy still lacking a functioning payout route. Meanwhile, the live sector shows massive potential as Fally Ipupa sets new records for Francophone music in London. Professionalizing registration remains the priority for the industry, from Morocco’s triple-entry requirements to Audiomack’s strict enrollment rules.

## Top stories
**Benin's Private Copy Levy Stalled:** Despite a 2015 decree establishing a private copy levy, a recent study by CISAC (International Confederation of Societies of Authors and Composers) shows no functioning payout chain for rights holders. Artists and labels still lack the necessary receipts and distribution rules to access this income. [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/benin-set-a-private-copy-levy-in-2015-where-is-the-payout-route/).

**Fally Ipupa's London Record:** The Congolese star's two-night run at The O2 in October marks the largest Francophone concert in UK history. The venue reports that 2026 is its most linguistically diverse year, with 18 languages represented on stage. [IQ Magazine](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/11/francophone-music-a-growing-sensation-after-largest-ever-uk-show/).

**Morocco's Registration Complexity:** Royalties in Morocco require three separate registrations with BMDAV (Bureau Marocain du Droit d'Auteur et des Droits Voisins). An ISRC (International Standard Recording Code) only identifies the recording, meaning the song, the performers, and the producer must be registered individually to reconcile all claims. [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/morocco-bmdav-song-performer-producer-registration-guide/).

## Streaming and platforms
**Audiomack AMP Enrollment Rules:** The Audiomack Monetization Program (AMP) does not offer back-pay for streams accumulated before a creator enrolls and activates their uploads. The platform uses a 50% revenue-share formula with a net-90 payment schedule. [The InterSpace Daily](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/audiomack-amp-activation-no-back-pay-direct-uploads/).

**Gospel Platform in DRC:** A new digital initiative, Gospel Talents du Grand Katanga, has launched to promote young religious artists in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The platform uses YouTube, Facebook, and WhatsApp to distribute video performances and facilitate audience voting. [ACP](https://acp.cd/culture/musique-une-plateforme-numerique-entend-promouvoir-les-artistes-gospel-dans-le-grand-katanga/).

**Nigeria Chart Moves:** Olamide's "Burukutu" debuted at No. 9 on the TurnTable Official Nigeria Top 100, showing stronger performance on radio than on streaming in its first week. ## Rights and regulation
**Copyright Damages in Uganda:** The High Court Commercial Division in Kampala ordered the estate of the late Justice George Kanyeihamba to pay 45 million Ugandan Shillings (approximately 12,000 USD) for copyright theft. The court found that the late judge used a researcher's original work in a law textbook without authorization. [Daily Monitor Uganda](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/court-orders-late-justice-kanyeihamba-s-estate-to-pay-shs45m-over-copyright-theft-5654234).

## Key takeaways
1. **Activate monetization early:** Platforms like Audiomack do not pay retroactively, so labels must ensure AMP enrollment is active before a release gains traction.
2. **Verify regional registration rules:** In markets like Morocco, an ISRC is insufficient for full royalty collection; separate filings for authors, performers, and producers are mandatory.
3. **Monitor Francophone live demand:** The record-breaking success of Fally Ipupa in London indicates a significant opportunity for Francophone African artists to headline major European arenas.
